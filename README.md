# 🚚📦 Purchase & Shipping Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

Developed an interactive Excel dashboard to analyze purchasing trends, shipping performance, product category distribution, seasonal demand patterns, payment preferences, and customer purchase behavior.

The dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor procurement activities, identify purchasing patterns, evaluate shipping frequencies, and support data-driven supply chain decisions through dynamic filters and visual analytics.

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Clothing accounted for the highest share of total purchases.
- Winter season recorded the highest quantity purchased.
- Female customers represented 46% of total purchases, indicating a balanced customer base.
- Large (L) and Medium (M) sizes dominated product demand.
- Credit Card transactions were the most preferred payment method.
- Shipping frequencies were distributed relatively evenly, with "Every 3 Months" showing the highest count.
- Purchase activity peaked during July, indicating strong mid-year demand.
